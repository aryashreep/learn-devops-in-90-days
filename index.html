🛠️ Academy Study Flow

Is academy ka main goal hai consistency aur hands-on implementation. Har din ka topic seekhne ke liye niche diye gaye steps follow karein:

1
Study Slide

Open the daily 🎓 Pro Module to view theory slides in Golu & Jagu Hinglish style.

2
Solve Lab

Click on the 🧪 Lab Session task list and perform hands-on configuration/commands.

3
Write Report

Create your solution file containing execution output and logs inside the daily solution directory.

4
Share Publicly

Post your key learnings on LinkedIn using #LearnDevOpsIn90Days to build your proof of work.
