What will we learn today?

Today we will combine 3 powerful concepts: `tar` for archiving, `find` for cleanup, and `cron` for scheduling. This is your first **Senior-Level Project**!

🎯 LEARNING OBJECTIVES
  • Package: Use `tar` and `gzip` to bundle logs into small archives.
  • Cleanup: Automatically find and delete files older than 7 days.
  • Schedule: Master the Crontab syntax for recurring automation.
  • Build: Create a production-ready `backup_manager.sh` script.
💡 ANALOGY: Cleaning the Warehouse

1. Logs: Raddi newspaper (Piling up every day).
2. Tar: Bundling.
3. Gzip: To injure Raddi by pressing it (Compression).
4. Crontab: Alarm clock which tells the cleaning person (Script) at 3 in the morning.
