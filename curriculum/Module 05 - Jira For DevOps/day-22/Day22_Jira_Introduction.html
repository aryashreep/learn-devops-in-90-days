🎯 LEARNING OBJECTIVES
  • Define: What is Jira and why is it the industry standard?
  • Choose: Understand different Jira project templates (Scrum/Kanban).
  • Navigate: Master the Jira Dashboard and Sidebar.
  • Deployment: Difference between Jira Cloud and Data Center.
❓ WHY JIRA FOR DEVOPS?

1. Transparency: Sabko dikhta hai kaun kya kar raha hai.
2. Integration: GitHub, Jenkins, aur Slack se seedha connect hota hai.
3. Agile: Sprints aur Backlogs manage karna bahut easy hai.
4. History: Har task ki poori history save rehti hai.

☁️ CLOUD VS 🏢 DATA CENTER

Jira Cloud

"Atlassian manage karta hai. Instant setup, no maintenance."

  • Modern UI.
  • SaaS model.
  • Best for: Most startups & mid-size teams.
VS

Data Center

"Aapke apne server pe chalta hai. Full control!"

  • Highly customizable.
  • Self-managed.
  • Best for: Giant enterprises with strict security.
